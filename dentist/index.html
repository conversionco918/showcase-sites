Most people who put off the dentist aren't scared of a cleaning. They're tired of being rushed, scolded and surprised by the bill. So those are the things we fixed first.

Seventy-five minutes, and here's how we spend them.

Surprises are the worst part of going to the dentist, so here is the whole first appointment, start to finish.

It has been years since my last visit. Will I get a lecture?

No. About a third of our new patients haven't seen a dentist in five years or more. We look, explain what we find, and help you sort out what matters now from what can safely wait.

Do you take my insurance?

We're in network with most PPO plans, including Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna, Guardian and United Concordia. Send your details when you book and we'll check your benefits before you arrive. We don't take TennCare or HMO plans.

What does the $129 new patient visit include?

A full exam with Dr. Ashford, a full set of digital X-rays, a routine cleaning, photos and a written treatment plan with prices. It's for new patients without insurance, when there's no active gum disease.

I get really anxious. What can you do?

Tell us when you book and we'll schedule extra time. You can meet Dr. Ashford before you sit in the chair, use headphones and a warm blanket, and agree on a hand signal to pause. Nitrous oxide and oral sedation are available after a health review.

When should my child first come in?

By their first birthday, or within six months of the first tooth. Early visits are short and mostly about getting comfortable with the room, the chair and the team.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Bad toothache, swelling, a broken or knocked-out tooth, or a lost crown. Call first thing and we'll hold a same-day spot when we can. If swelling affects your breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest emergency room.

Is the membership plan insurance?

No. It's a discount plan for care at Larkspur. You pay a flat monthly or yearly fee, routine care is included, and other treatment is 15 percent off. There are no deductibles, claim forms or yearly maximums.

Where do I park?

Free parking in our lot behind the studio, with a step-free entrance. If you're early, Sevier Park and the coffee shops along 12th Avenue are a short walk away.
