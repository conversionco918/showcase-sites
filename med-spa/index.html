Est. 2014 · Coral Gables

I opened Maison Vale in 2014 after a year of dissolving other people’s filler.

Miami has no shortage of med spas, and most of them are very good at selling. I wanted a studio that was good at saying no: no to a third syringe on a first visit, no to lasers in August, and no to treating someone who mostly needed sleep and a better sunscreen.

Twelve years in, our nurses and I have done a little over 31,000 appointments. About two out of three new patients are sent by someone who already comes here. That is the number I watch, because people only send their friends when the work doesn’t show.

You will pay a fair price here, and you will see it before you sit down. The menu below is the real one.

Laser season starts in December.

From April to September the UV index here sits near 10 most afternoons, and by about 35 it shows up as brown spots, redness and texture. So we plan around the sun. IPL and resurfacing happen when the UV drops by about half and a week indoors is realistic. Summer is for injectables, facials and good sunscreen.

    The consultation

    Free for injectables

Questions we get every week.

Will I look done?

Not if we can help it. First visits are dosed on the light side and checked at two weeks. Most people start with fewer units and less filler than they expected, and a good share of our new patients come to us to have older filler dissolved.

Who does my treatment?

A licensed provider, after a documented exam: Dr. Montoya, a nurse practitioner working under her written protocol, or a registered nurse under her supervision. Laser hair removal is done by nurses or licensed electrologists under physician supervision, which Florida requires.

Why does the consultation cost $100?

Because it’s 45 minutes with a provider, not a coordinator with a price sheet. The $100 comes off any treatment you book within 60 days, and consultations are free for members.

When should I book laser?

IPL and resurfacing work best from December to March, when the sun is lower and staying out of it for a week is realistic. Hair removal and RF microneedling run all year. Tell us about summer travel and we’ll plan around it.

How long does Botox last?

Three to four months for most people. It varies with the area, the dose and your metabolism, and a first treatment can wear off a little sooner.

Can you treat darker skin with laser?

Yes, with the right device and settings. Our hair removal laser is safe on all skin types, and we do a test spot before any first laser treatment.

Can I see someone in Spanish?

Yes. Dr. Montoya and Marco both speak Spanish. Mention it when you book and we’ll schedule your consultation with her.

Where do I park?

In the garage behind the building. We validate, so it’s free. Text us from the elevator and we’ll meet you at the side door.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes, after six months, with 30 days’ notice. Banked credit stays usable until your last billing date.
