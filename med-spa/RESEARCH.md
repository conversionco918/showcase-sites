# Research notes: Maison Vale Aesthetics (med spa concept, Coral Gables, Miami)

What I studied
- Homepage teardowns and live sites from the first build (Moda's six teardowns, WebCitz's 13-site audit, SkinSpirit, Ever/Body, Mirabile M.D.), plus AmSpa booking guidance and membership pages.
- Miami market pricing: Med Aesthetics Miami ($13.50 a unit Botox), Lumea Med Spa ($14), Aurelia ($11.50 promo), Miami Skin and Vein (physician injectors $15 to $17), and 2026 Miami and Coral Gables filler guides (lips $650 to $1,100 a syringe, dermal filler from about $750).
- Florida rules: med spa round-ups from MedSpaStandards, Portrait Care and Prospyr. A Florida-licensed MD or DO must act as medical director; APRNs inject under a written collaborative protocol; RNs inject as a delegated act under physician supervision; laser hair removal is limited to physicians, supervised nurses, PAs and licensed electrologists under physician supervision (estheticians, called facial specialists in Florida, may not do it). Florida also restricts how physicians advertise board certification, so the page names the certifying board.
- Miami sun data: summer UV index sits around 10 to 12, winter about half that, which is why dermatologists here push IPL and resurfacing into December to March.

What the best homepages do
- Order: booking-led hero, treatments with prices, proof, providers, reviews, location and booking. The primary action repeats after every major section.
- Trust leads with people: named providers with credentials and a plain medical director statement. Price transparency and written membership terms reduce consult no-shows.
- The fear to answer is looking overdone, which is sharper in Miami than almost anywhere.

Decisions it drove
- Positioning in the owner's voice: "We'll probably suggest less." The founder's letter carries the numbers (31,000 appointments, two in three new patients referred) inside sentences instead of a stat row.
- Miami pricing sits slightly above the middle for a physician-led studio: Botox $15 a unit, filler from $775, lips from $475 for a half syringe, a $100 consultation credited to treatment, memberships at $149, $249 and $449 set out as a table with written terms.
- Florida compliance on the page: medical director named with "certified by the American Board of Dermatology", APRN protocol and RN supervision stated, laser hair removal assigned to an electrologist and nurses, "results vary" beside reviews and in the footer, no before and after photos, and a note asking people not to put medical details in the form.
- Local detail that a real Coral Gables studio would mention: Mediterranean Revival building with a courtyard (it also suits the arch motif), validated garage parking, Spanish-speaking founder and front desk, hurricane-season closures, patients from Coconut Grove, Brickell, Pinecrest, Key Biscayne, South Miami and Coral Way.
- Name check: no med spa called Maison Vale in Florida or nationally. Nearby names that exist are unrelated (Maison Valentine, a Miami Beach restaurant; Maison & Co., a Coral Gables showroom; a "The Vale" med spa mockup by a web studio). Kept Maison Vale Aesthetics.
