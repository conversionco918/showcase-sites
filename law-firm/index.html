What we handle

Injury and wrongful death cases, and nothing else. No wills, no divorces, no traffic tickets. We only represent injured people and their families, never insurance companies.

  • Car wrecks Rear-end crashes on I-10 and I-45, intersection collisions, hit-and-runs and drivers with no insurance. We deal with the adjusters so you can get on with getting better.
  • 18-wheeler crashes Trucks hauling containers out of the Port of Houston run on federal rules for driver hours, maintenance and logbooks. We send preservation letters within days, before that data is gone.
  • Motorcycle crashes Riders are often blamed first. We rebuild the crash from scene measurements, vehicle data and witnesses to show what actually happened.
  • Wrongful death Texas law lets a spouse, children and parents recover for lost income, medical and funeral costs, and the loss of the person they loved.
  • Workplace injuries Texas lets employers opt out of workers' compensation. If yours did, you can sue them directly. If a contractor or equipment maker caused the injury, you may have a claim against them too.
  • Slip and fall Stores and property owners have to deal with hazards they know about. We move quickly to get camera footage before it is recorded over.
  • Dog bites Texas has no blanket dog bite statute, so these cases turn on what the owner knew and how careful they were. We find that out early.
  • Pedestrians and cyclists Crosswalk and bike lane crashes, including cases against rideshare drivers and delivery fleets.

The first week after a wreck

Texas gives most injured people two years to file a lawsuit. The evidence that decides the case starts disappearing in days.

  • See a doctor, even if you feel fine.

    Some injuries take days to show. A gap in treatment is the first thing an adjuster looks for.

  • Use your own PIP coverage.

    Most Texas auto policies include personal injury protection unless you turned it down in writing. It pays medical bills and lost wages no matter who caused the crash.

  • Skip the recorded statement.

    You don't have to give one to the other driver's insurance company, and what you say can be used to reduce your claim.

  • Call us before you sign anything.

    A first offer often arrives before anyone knows what your injuries will cost. Once you sign a release, the claim is closed.

How much does it cost to hire you?

Nothing up front. We work on a contingent fee: 33⅓% of the recovery if your case settles before a lawsuit is filed, 40% after. If we don't recover money for you, there is no fee and you owe nothing for expenses. The full breakdown is in the fees section above.

How long do I have to file a claim in Texas?

For most injury and wrongful death cases, two years. Claims against a city, county or state agency are different: written notice is often due within six months, and some cities require it sooner. If a government vehicle or a road defect was involved, call early.

What if the wreck was partly my fault?

You may still have a case. Texas follows proportionate responsibility. If you are 50 percent or less at fault, you can recover damages, reduced by your share of the fault.

Should I talk to the other driver's insurance company?

Report the wreck to your own insurer as your policy requires. You are not required to give a recorded statement to the other driver's insurer, and we recommend you don't. Once you hire us, every call goes through our office.

I was hurt at work. Can I sue my employer?

It depends on whether your employer carries workers' compensation. Texas lets employers opt out, and if yours is a nonsubscriber you can sue them for negligence. If they do carry it, workers' comp is usually your only remedy against them, but you may still have a claim against a contractor, driver or equipment maker who caused the injury.

Will my case go to trial?

Probably not. Most injury cases settle. We still prepare every case as if it will be tried, because that is what moves an insurer toward a fair offer. If yours does go to trial, you will be ready for every step, and the decision to settle is always yours.

How long will my case take?

Most of our cases resolve in 9 to 18 months. The biggest factor is your medical treatment. We usually wait until your doctors can describe your long-term outlook, so the settlement covers what you will actually need.
