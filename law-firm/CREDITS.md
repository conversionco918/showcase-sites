# Photo credits

All photos are from Pexels (free licence, commercial use allowed, no attribution required). Credited here anyway.
Every file was downloaded from the Pexels CDN, checked by eye, cropped where noted, given one shared cool, higher-contrast grade with ImageMagick, and saved as webp.

The six office photos come from one Pexels law-office session (photo IDs 4427xxx). Page titles and photographer names were not visible to us because the Pexels site blocks scripted access, so "Photographer" is listed as "see page" where we could not confirm it.
