What people come in for

Back pain often starts at the hips. Headaches often start in the neck. We test the whole chain before we treat anything, so you are not paying to chase symptoms.

Low back pain

Stiff mornings, pain after sitting, the twinge when you load the car for the mountains. Our most common visit.

Neck pain and stiffness

Screens, steering wheels and sleeping wrong. We work on the joints and the muscles that guard them.

Tension headaches

Headaches that build from the neck and shoulders. We screen first and send you to a physician if something needs a medical look.

Sciatica and leg pain

Pain that travels into the hip, leg or foot. We find where it starts before we decide how to treat it.

Running and riding injuries

Hips, knees, IT band and low back trouble that shows up as the miles go up.

Ski and climbing strains

Tweaked backs from a hard fall, sore shoulders from too many nights on the wall.

Pregnancy back pain

Gentle care for the low back and pelvis, with pregnancy pillows and a drop table.

Auto accident injuries

Whiplash and soreness after a crash, documented properly and billed to your auto insurer.

Chiropractic care focuses on muscles, joints and the spine. If your symptoms point somewhere else, we will tell you and help you find the right provider.

How we work

No mystery cracking and no one-size routine. Every technique we use has a reason, and you will hear that reason before we start.

Adjustments

Precise, gentle adjustments to the spine, hips and shoulders. We can do them by hand or with an instrument, and you choose.

Most people feel pressure, then relief. A little soreness the next day is normal.

Soft tissue work

Focused work on the tight, guarded muscles around the sore joint. It helps the adjustment hold and makes moving feel easier.

Included with most visits. Jo, our massage therapist, takes the longer sessions.

Rehab you will actually do

Two or three exercises matched to your goal, checked and progressed every visit. This is the part that keeps you out of our office.

You leave every visit with a short home plan on your phone.

Your first visit

What happens in the first hour

You leave knowing what is going on, what it will take and what it costs. No surprises at the front desk.

  1. 0:00

    Your story

    You fill out your history online before you arrive. Then we sit down and talk about what hurts, when it started and what you want to get back to.

  2. 0:15

    The exam

    Posture, range of motion, orthopedic and neurological tests. If the exam calls for X-rays, we take them on site that day.

  3. 0:35

    Your plan, in plain English

    What we found, how many visits it should take and what it costs. If chiropractic is not the right fit, we say so and refer you.

  4. 0:45

    First adjustment and homework

    If you are ready, care starts today. You leave with two or three exercises and your next visit booked.

What to bring: clothes you can move in, your insurance card and a list of any medications. Running late? Call us and we will adjust.

Does an adjustment hurt?

Most people feel pressure and then relief. Some feel a little sore for a day, like after a new workout. If you do not like the classic “pop”, we use gentler instrument and drop table techniques instead.

Do I need a referral?

No. In Colorado you can book with a chiropractor directly. A few insurance plans ask for one, and we check that when we verify your benefits.

How many visits will I need?

It depends on what we find. Most first care plans run 6 to 8 visits over three or four weeks, then we re-test. You will know the number before you commit to anything.

Will I have to keep coming forever?

No. The goal is to get you moving well and teach you how to keep it that way. Some patients choose a monthly tune-up before ski season or a big race. That is optional.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept most major plans and check your benefits for free before your first visit. If you do not have coverage, our self-pay prices are listed above, and HSA or FSA cards work too.

Can I come in while pregnant?

Yes. We use pregnancy pillows, a drop table and gentle techniques, and we coordinate with your OB or midwife when your history calls for it.

Do you X-ray every patient?

No. We only take X-rays when your history and exam call for them. When we do, they are digital, taken on site and included in your new patient visit.

Where do I park?

Street parking is free for two hours on our block, and there is a bike rack out front. Plenty of patients walk over the river from Union Station.
