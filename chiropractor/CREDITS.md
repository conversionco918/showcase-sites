Notes
- The Yan Krukau clinic photos come from one session (Pexels IDs 5793800 to 5794060), so the room, the light and the "doctor" match across the page. The photographer was confirmed from the EXIF copyright field on the Pexels CDN files ("Krukphoto.com") and from Pexels search listings for other photos in the same set.
- The hero and three other photos (14797752, 14797754, 14797755, 14797760) come from a second session in one chiropractic clinic, found by checking the IDs next to "A Doctor Explaining a Diagnosis To a Man" (14797760). The same chiropractor appears in all four, so on the page he reads as the clinic's second doctor. Their CDN files carry only "Copyright 2022. All rights reserved." in EXIF (the Pexels licence still applies to the Pexels upload), so the photographer name is left blank rather than guessed. Framed certificates and charts on the walls were blurred so no text is readable.
- The four mountain-hiking photos (7893093, 7893095, 7893099, 7893122) come from one outdoor session on Pexels. The CDN files for them and for the other outdoor photos carry no usable EXIF credit, and the Pexels site blocks scripted page fetches, so those photographer names are left blank too. Open each page URL to read the name.
- In the skyline photo (16108565) the tower logo was replaced with a patch of the same glass facade, and the smaller building signs were inpainted, so no company name is readable.
- Bare-ID page URLs (`/photo/<ID>/`) redirect to the full slug on Pexels.
- Everyone pictured is a stock model. The names used on the site (Dr. Lena Hartwell, staff, patients) are fictional.
